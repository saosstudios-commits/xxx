FAQ

Questions Ghanaian sellers ask us

Do I need a website or a developer to use SeePay?
No. SeePay gives you a hosted storefront with your own shop link. You manage products and orders from the dashboard — no designing, hosting or coding required.
How do my buyers pay me?
Your storefront checkout shows the payment methods you have configured — for example MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash or AT Money. Each provider becomes selectable only after SeePay has set it up and verified it for your store, so buyers never choose a method that cannot actually collect money.
Can I keep selling on WhatsApp while using SeePay?
Yes — that is exactly how most sellers start. Share your SeePay shop link in chats and statuses; when someone is ready to buy, checkout is one clean page instead of a long conversation.
How will I know an order is really paid?
SeePay marks a payment as paid only after confirmation from the payment provider or your backend — never from a screenshot or buyer message. Orders without verified payment stay clearly marked as awaiting payment.
What do agents actually do?
Agents share a referral link and earn GH₵50 for each eligible shop activation after the GH₵150 activation payment is verified.
How much does SeePay cost?
See the pricing page for our GH₵150 one-time activation fee, GH₵0.20 per successful paid order, and Paystack’s separate processing fee. There is no monthly subscription.
