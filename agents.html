How it works

From referral link to confirmed commission

The SeePay agent dashboard shows your real activity. Nothing flashes “guaranteed income” here — the programme's value is clear work, clearly tracked.

1

Register and get verified

Fill the agent registration form. The SeePay team reviews your onboarding. Earn GH₵50 for each eligible referred shop activation after its GH₵150 setup fee is verified.

2

Share your referral link

Talk to the seamstresses, food sellers, pharmacies and shops you already know. Your link records who registers through you.

3

Earn, transparently

Your portal lists every referred merchant. Earn GH₵50 for each eligible completed shop activation; your portal tracks referral activity and earned commission. Your payout history — portal preview.

Who becomes a SeePay agent

The programme is open to people and businesses who genuinely serve Ghanaian merchants — registrations are reviewed by the SeePay team.

  • Contactable in GhanaA working Ghana phone number and an email we can reach you on.
